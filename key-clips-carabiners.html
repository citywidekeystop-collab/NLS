Choosing a clip for keys and bags

A key clip can connect a key set to a suitable bag loop or keep small accessories together. A screw-lock gate and a braided cord with clip provide different attachment styles. Compare the product photographs and the listed size; the aluminum screw-lock option is described as 8 cm. These clips and cords are everyday accessories. They are not climbing equipment and must not be used for lifting, fall protection, towing or other load-bearing tasks. Check that the gate closes fully, avoid overloading the attachment and replace worn or damaged accessories.
