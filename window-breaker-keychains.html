Choosing a car escape keychain

A compact escape tool can stay attached to car keys or another accessible place in a vehicle. Compare the listed functions: a window-breaking tip, a seat-belt cutting slot, or an added whistle. Before choosing a tool, check your vehicle’s glass markings. These listings are for compatible tempered side glass, not windshields or laminated glass. Read and follow the product instructions; carrying a tool does not establish that it is compatible with every window. Keep the cutter slot covered when the product is not in use and store the tool where it will not interfere with driving.
