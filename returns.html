Return & Refund Policy

Effective date: October 12, 2026

National Locks Supply (NLS) sells residential and commercial locks, door hardware and security products. The following policy explains how to request a return, exchange or refund. Nothing in this policy limits any rights you have under applicable law.

1. Return window and eligibility

Customers may request a return within 30 calendar days of delivery. For change-of-mind returns, the item must be unused, uninstalled, unmodified and in its original packaging, with all keys, accessories, components and instructions.

2. Items not eligible for change-of-mind returns

Custom-keyed, keyed-alike, master-keyed, specially ordered or personalized items generally cannot be returned once customized or ordered for you. Installed, used or altered locks and electronic locks that have been activated or modified are generally not eligible for change-of-mind returns. These restrictions do not apply when a return, replacement or refund is required by law for defective, damaged or incorrectly supplied goods.

3. Damaged, defective or incorrect orders

Contact NLS as soon as possible, preferably within 7 days of delivery, if your item arrives damaged, defective or incorrect. Provide your order number, details of the issue and photographs if available. We will review the issue and offer an appropriate remedy, such as a replacement or refund. NLS covers reasonable return shipping when we shipped an incorrect or defective item.

4. How to start a return

Call 443-578-1686 to request return authorization. Have your order number, item name, delivery date and reason for return ready. We will provide instructions and the return address. Do not mail items back without return authorization.

5. Return shipping and restocking fees

For eligible change-of-mind returns, the customer pays return shipping. Original outbound shipping is generally nonrefundable for change-of-mind returns, unless required by law. There is no restocking fee for eligible standard returns. NLS covers reasonable return shipping for our fulfillment errors or defective items.

6. Refunds

We inspect received returns and notify you whether the refund is approved. Approved refunds are sent to the original payment method within 10 business days after return approval. Your financial institution may take additional time to post the credit.

7. Order cancellation

Contact us promptly if you need to cancel an order. Orders can generally be canceled before shipment or fulfillment begins. Once shipped, the return process applies. Custom and special-order items may not be cancelable after work or procurement has started.

8. Product warranties

Where applicable, manufacturer or product-specific warranties are explained with the product or its packaging. Warranty coverage is separate from this return policy and does not reduce statutory consumer rights.
