Choosing a whistle for your key ring

A whistle keychain combines a small whistle with an attachment for a key ring or bag. A round tube, slim tube and flat double-tube design each offer a different shape for everyday carry. Start with how you plan to attach it, then compare the photos and the number of pieces in the selected option. A two-pack contains two matching pieces, while a single-piece listing includes one whistle. These products have no measured loudness rating listed here. Keep small parts away from young children and inspect the ring and attachment for wear during normal use.
