Choosing a small key accessory

Small key accessories can help organize or carry everyday items. Compare a round compass attached to a ring, a plastic compass clip and a screw-top capsule for small dry items. The compass products are novelty accessories and are not substitutes for reliable navigation equipment. Clips are not for climbing, lifting or fall protection. Capsule listings do not include contents, and no waterproof or child-resistant rating is claimed. Check the selected pack quantity: a two-pack includes two matching pieces. Match the accessory to its intended everyday use and inspect its ring, closure or clip periodically.
