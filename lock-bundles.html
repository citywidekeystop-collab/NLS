Before you order

Will these locks fit my doors?

Confirm door thickness, backset, bore sizes and spacing between the knob and deadbolt before ordering. Contact NLS if you need help matching hardware.

Is installation included?

Prices are for the hardware bundle and selected keying configuration. Installation is separate.

How do I order?

Choose a style, finish, bundle size and keying option, then select Order This Bundle. Review your selections and continue to secure Stripe checkout to enter your shipping and payment details.

How much is shipping?

Standard shipping for qualifying orders to the contiguous United States is $9 for merchandise subtotals under $100 and free at $100 or more. Shipping and any applicable tax are shown before payment. Contact NLS before ordering for Alaska, Hawaii or special-handling deliveries.
