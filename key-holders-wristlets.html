Choosing a key holder or wrist strap

Choose a carrying style around the items you want to keep together. A wrist strap or decorative loop can make a small key set easier to pick out of a bag. A lanyard offers a longer carrying strap, while a coin-purse keychain adds a small zippered pouch. Compare the product photographs and listed color or pattern rather than assuming that similar names mean identical designs. Rhinestone pieces are decorative accessories. Avoid overloading their loops or clips, and inspect attachment points for wear. Coins, badges and other pictured contents are not included unless the product description explicitly lists them.
