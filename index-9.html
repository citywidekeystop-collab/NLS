Before you call

Questions about your locks?

Do you handle house and car lockouts in Bel Air?

Yes. Call with your exact location and the type of door or vehicle. We’ll confirm current availability and service details.

Can you sell me a keypad lock and install it?

Yes. Ask about keypad and compatible Wi-Fi lock options with mobile installation. A photo of your door helps us discuss fit, hardware, and setup.

Should I rekey or change the locks after moving?

Rekeying changes which key operates compatible existing hardware. Replacement is useful for damaged locks, new finishes, or a different function. We can discuss both based on the condition of your locks.

Do your locks come with a warranty?

Ask for the warranty included with your selected hardware and installation. Coverage, duration, and exclusions are confirmed with your quote.

Do you work on commercial doors and mailbox locks?

Yes. Contact us for mailbox lock replacement, business rekeying, storefront locks, lock repairs, panic bars, door closers, and access control options.

How much will service cost?

Pricing depends on your location, lock or vehicle type, hardware, and the work required. Call or text details and photos to request pricing.

Are you available at night or on weekends?

Citywide offers 24/7 locksmith service. Call to confirm availability for your location and the service you need.
