Smart Lock Questions

Does every keypad smart lock connect to Wi-Fi?

No. Some use Bluetooth, Z-Wave, or a separate hub. Confirm the model's connectivity before buying.

Can I buy a smart lock and request professional installation?

Yes. You can purchase a lock separately and contact us for a local installation quote. Pricing, scheduling and technician availability are confirmed individually.

Can you install a lock I already purchased?

You can request a quote for customer-supplied hardware. Compatibility and installation requirements must be checked first.

Do you guarantee a licensed locksmith will be available?

No. We will confirm the assigned provider's qualifications and any applicable licensing requirements before scheduling. Availability is not guaranteed.

Do smart locks work during a Wi-Fi outage?

Many allow local entry by keypad, physical key, or other offline method, but this depends on the model. Remote functions may stop during an outage.
